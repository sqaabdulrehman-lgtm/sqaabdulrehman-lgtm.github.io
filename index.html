A little about me

Quality is more than finding bugs.

I focus on how software behaves in real-world scenarios: where workflows break, where users get confused, where business rules are at risk, and where small defects can create larger problems.

“My goal is not simply to report defects. It is to help teams make confident decisions, reduce risk, and release software that works for people and for the business.”

I bring a practical, investigative mindset to every project: understanding client requirements, supporting project planning, identifying risks early, and checking that requirements move smoothly from planning through development, testing, and release. I aim for fast deliveries without compromising quality, while keeping clients informed and satisfied.
